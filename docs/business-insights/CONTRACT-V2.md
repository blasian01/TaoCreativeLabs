## Mandatory evidence, not inferred eligibility

`Snapshot.schemaVersion` is2. New Evidence fields: environment production/sandbox/unknown; authority direct_aggregate/provider_native/owner_source/historical_report/synthetic_fixture; precision exact/rounded/unknown; maturity complete/pending/unknown/not_applicable; IANA timezone; source dataCompleteThrough watermark; extractedAt distinct from nullable original observedAt. Every field is required even when its value is unknown/null. Null original observation requires explanation/nonmeasured coverage and yields unknown freshness, never retrieval-time freshness. UTC source timestamps accept1–6fractional digits and preserve microsecond strings. Unknown original business window uses `kind:unknown,reason` with nonmeasured coverage; never create a fictitious snapshot timestamp.

Coverage adds suppressed/pending_maturity/not_applicable to measured/partial/missing/blocked/unavailable. Missing/blocked/unavailable/suppressed/pending/not-applicable require null; measured requires numeric value. Partial source values can be displayed but cannot certify normalized totals/rates. Complete measured periods require source watermark through their end. Normalized measured business metrics require exact precision and production environment. Raw/native source observations can have unknown environment without becoming business totals.

New entity distinguishes account/source_account/installation/customer/subscription_chain/seat/provider_customer/download/device; null for money. Units are not interchangeable. `paid_subscribers` means paid subscription chains, paying_customers distinct customers, paid_seats seats, active_trials trial chains. Cohort numerator/denominator must match entity/source/scope/window/timezone/currency and complete maturity. `pending_maturity`, `suppressed`, `undefined` zero denominator, `unavailable` and `incomparable` remain distinct result states. No owner suppression threshold invented.

## Conservative money arithmetic

Source values must be safe integer counts/minor units. Decimal provider values must be losslessly converted upstream; unsupported precision/overflow is not silently rounded. monthlyRecurring computes **BigInt rational** numerator/denominator; `Result.exact` stores their strings. annualRecurring uses the exact fraction×12. Monetary portfolio sums and receipts also use integer arithmetic. `value` is for numeric presentation, not a substitute for exact monetary arithmetic; retain exact fractions in derived exports/storage and round only for presentation. Do not convert a fractional normalization into a rounded source Observation and then sum it.

RecurringGroup adds precision exact/rounded/unknown. Actual verified aggregate contracted recurring consideration is already quantity-adjusted once, discounted and ex-tax/fees. List-price/rounded/unknown source amount cannot certify MRR. Catalog must have production scope and verified product identity. Annual/12, quarterly/3, multi-year/12N. Weekly Signal convention52/12 requires `{weeksPerYear:52,evidence:definitionRef}`; alternative calendar-day policy365–366must be explicitly evidenced with `{daysPerYear,evidence}`. No silent convention default.

Paid-through scheduled cancellation remains included. Trial, expired, unpaid, **grace_after_paid_through**, refunded, revoked, lifetime/packs/nonrecurring are excluded from conservative MRR when exclusion evidence is complete. Separate at_risk_entitled_mrr retains post-paid-through entitled grace exposure. Refund cash never blindly subtracts annual collections from normalized monthly MRR. Net receipts can be negative; source-side tax/refund/dispute dedup still required. Fees/payouts and recognized accounting revenue are not implemented as receipts aliases.

portfolioSum requires complete reviewed dedup/disjointness proof over exact IDs, production/exact/full coverage and compatible units/entity/scope/windows/currencies. Default expected app coverage is all five; a selected subset must be explicitly declared in proof.apps. RC+store purchase-chain duplication and web/mobile overlap reconcile privately upstream; no default unique-person total across apps or mixed-currency/FX sum. Proof is an audited source reference, not an arbitrary UI checkbox.
